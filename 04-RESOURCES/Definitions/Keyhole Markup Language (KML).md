## Overview

**Keyhole Markup Language**, or **KML** is an [[Extensible Markup Lanaguage (XML)|XML]]-based file format used to display geographic data on 2D/3D maps, and was originally developed by Keyhole, Inc. which was acquired by Google and in turn leveraged by Google Earth.
