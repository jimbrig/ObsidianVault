## Overview

A **Census Block Group** is a geographic unit used by the [[U.S. Census Bureau]] that is a subdivision of a [[census tract]] and typically contains between 600 and 3,000 people. Block groups are the smallest geographic unit for which the Census Bureau publishes sample data from the [[American Community Survey (ACS)]].

## Geographic Hierarchy

```plaintext
State
└── County
    └── Census Tract
        └── Block Group (1-9 per tract)
            └── Census Block (many per BG)
```
