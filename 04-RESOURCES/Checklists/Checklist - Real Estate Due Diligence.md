### Physical / Environmental

- Phase I Environmental Site Assessment (ESA)
- Geotechnical investigation (soil bearing capacity, groundwater)
- ALTA/NSPS survey
- Flood zone determination (FEMA FIRM maps)
- Wetlands delineation
- Topographic survey and grading analysis

### Legal / Regulatory

- Title search and title insurance commitment
- Zoning verification letter
- Easement and encumbrance review
- Entitlement status and conditions of approval
- Utility availability letters

### Financial

- Market study / absorption analysis
- Construction cost estimates
- Pro forma financial modeling
- Tax assessment review
- Impact fee verification
